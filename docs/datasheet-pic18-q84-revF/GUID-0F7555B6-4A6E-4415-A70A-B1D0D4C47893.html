14.2.2 BOR Is Off in Sleep

When the BOREN Configuration bits are programmed to ‘b10, the BOR is on, except in Sleep. The device start-up will be delayed until the BOR is ready and VDD is higher than the BOR threshold.

BOR protection is not active during Sleep. The device wake-up will be delayed until the BOR is ready.
