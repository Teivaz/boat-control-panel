28.9.10 TUCHAIN

Timer Chain Control
Note: When chained, TUxyCON0, TUxyCON1, TUxyHLT, TUxyPS, TUxyCLK and TUxyERS of the Secondary module are undefined. Refer to the Chaining Counter Timers section for details.
Name: TUCHAIN
Address: 0x3BB

Bit 76543210 
        CH16AB 
Access R/W 
Reset x 

Bit 0 – CH16AB Timers TU16A and TU16B Chain Enable

Reset States: 
POR/BOR = x
All Other Resets = u
ValueDescription
1 Timers TU16A (Main) and TU16B (Secondary) operate as a single 32-bit timer.

TU16ATMR, TU16ACR and TU16APR form the Least Significant bits of the counter, capture and period values, respectively.
0 Timers TU16A and TU16B operate as independent 16-bit timers
When chained, TUxyCON0, TUxyCON1, TUxyHLT, TUxyPS, TUxyCLK and TUxyERS of the Secondary module are undefined. Refer to the Chaining Counter Timers section for details.
