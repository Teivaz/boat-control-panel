8.5.15 CRC Polynomial

The Polynomial for the CRC of the non-boot block segments of memory
Note: The CRC-on-boot module uses a 32-bit polynomial, as such the polynomial configuration spans from CONFIG24 to CONFIG27, with the MSB of CONFIG24 being the XOR of polynomial term X31 and the LSB of CONFIG27 being the XOR of polynomial term X0.
