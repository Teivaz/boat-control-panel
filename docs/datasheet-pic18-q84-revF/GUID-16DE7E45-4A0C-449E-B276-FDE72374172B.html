14.2.1 BOR Is Always On

When the BOREN Configuration bits are programmed to ‘b11, the BOR is always on. The device start-up will be delayed until the BOR is ready and VDD is higher than the BOR threshold.

BOR protection is active during Sleep. The BOR does not delay wake-up from Sleep.
